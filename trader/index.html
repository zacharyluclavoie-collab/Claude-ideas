👋 New here? How it works

What it does. The bot looks at thousands of new crypto coins, skips scams and weak ones, buys the few that look strong, and sells them by itself. All money here is pretend (paper) money: nothing real is bought.

Desks. Each desk is its own copy of the bot with its own $2,000, trading one part of the market (Solana, Base, BSC, Arbitrum, or big established coins). This board adds them all up.

Best mode. ★ Lots of trades is the best setting found in testing (on 8 demo markets it never saw: about +7.5% a day, every one made money). The badge on the blue card tells you if every desk uses it.

🤖 Full Auto (on by default) runs everything for you: it keeps every desk on the best mode with the tested settings, uses the biggest market your device handles, restarts desks after a bad day, and restarts a desk that freezes. On the real market it also warns about failing data and quiet desks, and tells you when your balance is too small to split across desks. You do not need to change any settings.

Wins and losses. About half the coins lose a little: that is normal. The bot sells a loser at about −4.5% (stop-loss), sells 30% of a winner at +6% and lets the rest run up to +30%, so wins are bigger than losses. Watch Avg win / avg loss and the Total profit, not single coins.

Safety checks. Every coin is checked for scam signs (can't sell, hidden owner powers, fake trading, too few real buyers). Trades whose fees would eat too much are skipped. If a desk loses too much in a day it stops until tomorrow.

Numbers. Open now: profit on coins still held. Per hour / per day: average so far. Market size: how many coins the demo creates (Auto picks the biggest your device runs smoothly). Live scanner: what the desks are checking right now.

Small balances. Every trade pays network and exchange fees (on new Solana coins about $0.17 for a buy plus a sell). On a $2 trade that is 8%, so with $10 the bot almost never buys. In tests $25–$50 came out about even and $100 or more made money. Start with $100 of pretend money: Full view → Master settings → Small start.

Demo vs live. Demo is a realistic simulated market and can run fast. Live (laptop version) uses real coin prices in real time, still with pretend money. Results on real coins will likely be lower than in the demo. To find out, run the real-price test on a laptop: double-click START REAL-PRICE TEST in the folder (or Full view → Master settings → Start the real-price test) and judge it after 100+ trades and 1-2 weeks.

📐 How the numbers work

One clock. The Master runs one simulated clock (Day N · hh:mm). Every desk is always at exactly that time, so all desks turn over to a new day together, and every number on this page comes from the same simulated moment ("As of" under the total).

Trade. A coin that was bought (the buy went through) and then sold (the sell went through) or written off by the logged rule. A failed buy is never a trade. Win rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses), rounded to a whole %.

Trades today / made / lost today. Trades closed during the current simulated day (Day N, midnight to midnight on the one clock). They reset only at simulated midnight; each reset is written in the activity log.

Last hour / last 24 hours. Change in balance over the last 1 / 24 simulated hours. Per hour / per day. Total profit divided by the simulated time the run has been going.

Open now. Profit or loss on coins still held, including any part already sold. Failed and retried. Every buy and sell attempt; fees burned on failed ones are inside each desk's profit.

Speed. "Selected" is the speed you chose; "actual" is measured: simulated seconds that really passed per real second over the last 10 seconds. All desks share one browser thread, so the actual speed depends on the device.

Paused vs No new trades. Paused stops the clock: nothing on the page changes. No new trades keeps the clock running and lets open coins be sold, but buys nothing new. Emergency stop freezes everything (sells nothing) until you tap Run twice.

Saving. The run is saved in this browser every few seconds and whenever you pause; after a reload you can Resume or Start over. Export makes a file you can import later.
