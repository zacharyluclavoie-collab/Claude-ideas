Paper Trader

Practice trading with fake money. It can never touch real money: there is no wallet inside.

🧪 Test it on real prices (recommended before any real money)

Runs one bot on real coin prices (Solana, Base, BSC, Arbitrum) with $100 of pretend money. Nothing real is ever bought. Judge it after 100+ trades and 1-2 weeks: the demo market is easier than the real one.

  1. Install Node.js (free, the LTS version) from nodejs.org. Only once.
  2. Double-click START REAL-PRICE TEST.command (Mac; the first time, right-click it and choose Open) or START REAL-PRICE TEST.bat (Windows).
  3. Leave the window open and the computer plugged in and awake. Your results page opens at http://localhost:8787/. It shows profit, trades, win rate, fees and a plain verdict.
  4. Every trade and an hourly summary are saved in More/real-test/ (real_test_trades.csv, real_test_hourly.csv). Send those two files back to Claude to tune the bot on real data.

No install? Open Paper Trader, tap Full, then Master settings → Start the real-price test. It only runs while that browser tab stays open.

1Start it

Open Paper Trader
Or double-click START PAPER TRADER.bat (Windows) or START PAPER TRADER.command (Mac) in this folder. The app opens in your browser. It trades while that browser tab stays open.

2Check it can see the market

  1. Tap Settings at the bottom.
  2. Tap Test data connections.
  3. You want four green ticks. A red one means that data source is blocked on your network.

3Watch it work

  • Home: your fake balance, a chart and every open position.
  • Positions: each coin it holds, with profit and loss.
  • Scanner: why it bought or skipped each coin. The top card Why isn't it trading? says what is blocking it, if anything.
  • Settings: how active it is, how strict the safety checks are, and how many positions to hold.

4If it isn't trading

  1. Give it 10 minutes after starting.
  2. Open Scanner and tap Copy diagnostic report.
  3. Paste it to Claude. It shows exactly what is blocking trades.

Want it to keep trading with the browser closed?

Install Node.js from nodejs.org, then double-click More\Start 24-7 (needs Node.js).bat. A status page opens at http://localhost:8787. Leave the black window open.
Important: fake money results do not predict real results. In testing this bot is roughly break-even at best, and trading more makes it lose more.
