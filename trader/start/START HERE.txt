PAPER TRADER - START HERE   (simulated money only, no wallet)

1) Double-click  "Start Paper Trader (app).bat"   (Mac: the .command file)
   Opens the full app in your browser with live market data.
   It trades only while that browser tab stays open.
   First thing to do: Settings > "Test data connections" and check all four lines are green.

2) For trading all day with the tab closed:
   Install Node.js (https://nodejs.org), then double-click "Start Paper Trader (runs 24-7)".
   A status page opens at http://localhost:8787/ . Leave the black window open.

If nothing trades: open the Scanner tab. The top card "Why isn't it trading?" says exactly what is blocking it.
